Good to Know

Questions, answered honestly

Can't find what you need? Message us — a real person will reply.

Your safety matters

dooo will never ask for your card number, bank PIN or OTP. You pay the courier only after checking your order. Only trust our official number +94 76 140 0696.

Do I have to pay before delivery?

No. You pay the delivery person only after you've opened and checked your order. If you're not satisfied, you can return it to the delivery company.

How do I place an order?

Note the design number, then send it with your size and colour to us on WhatsApp (+94 76 140 0696). We confirm the details and price, then dispatch it to you.

Do you deliver to my area?

Yes — we deliver islandwide across Sri Lanka. We'll confirm the delivery timing when you order.

What sizes are available?

Our trousers come in waist sizes 28" to 46". Tops and frocks come in M, L, XL, 2XL and 3XL. Unsure which fits? Send us your measurements and we'll help you choose.

Will the product look like the photos?

Every product is listed with its design number so you receive exactly the style you ordered — and you can check it before paying.

Do you accept wholesale orders?

Yes, both wholesale and retail orders are welcome. Message us on WhatsApp for wholesale pricing.
