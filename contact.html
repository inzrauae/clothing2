Your safety matters

dooo will never ask for your card number, bank PIN or OTP. You pay the courier only after checking your order. Only trust our official number +94 76 140 0696.

Do I have to pay before delivery?

No. Payment is cash on delivery, and you can open and inspect your order before you pay.

How do I place an order?

Choose your size and colour, add the piece to your bag and check out with your delivery details. We'll confirm your order on WhatsApp and dispatch it. You can also send us the design number, size and colour directly on WhatsApp.

Do you deliver to my area?

Yes. We offer fast islandwide delivery across Sri Lanka, and we'll confirm the delivery timing when you order.

Can I return or exchange an item?

Yes. Easy returns and exchanges are available for eligible items, including exchanges for size issues or product defects. See how it works.

What if my item is damaged or incorrect?

We offer a hassle-free exchange or money-back guarantee for eligible purchases. Just message us with your order details.

What sizes are available?

Our trousers come in waist sizes 28" to 46". Tops, frocks and sets come in M, L, XL, 2XL and 3XL. Unsure? Send us your measurements and we'll help you choose.

Do you accept wholesale orders?

Yes, both wholesale and retail orders are welcome. Message us on WhatsApp for wholesale pricing.
